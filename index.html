Showing active earnings that count up, live

Nine ways to show what a user is earning right now on pledged KAU. Each one is shown on desktop and mobile side by side, and every number is driven by the same live accrual model. Use the time controls to fast-forward: at 1 day/s a monthly payout lands about every 30 seconds.

01

Live earnings card

Keep the card you have today, but give Active earnings enough decimal places to visibly move. Add a pulse, a daily rate and a countdown to the next payout.

Low effort
Desktop · 1200
Mobile · 390Scroll inside the phone

Why it works

  • Closest to the current design, so it's the fastest to ship.
  • At 8 dp the number ticks about once a second. At 6 dp (today) it changes only every ~95 s, so it looks frozen.
  • Trailing digits are dimmed, so the stable part reads first and the motion stays calm.
  • The progress bar answers the next question users have: "when do I get it?"

Watch-outs

  • 8 decimals is wide on mobile. The type shrinks and lifetime earnings collapse to a single row.
  • "Active" vs "Lifetime" needs a one-line definition in the tooltip.
02

Odometer hero

Rolling digits on a dark gold card. The yield is turned into concrete amounts per hour, day, month and year, so earning feels real.

Medium effort
Desktop · 1200
Mobile · 390Scroll inside the phone

Why it works

  • The strongest emotional moment. Watching gold accrue is the product's promise.
  • The rate breakdown turns "5.08% APY" into money a user can picture.
  • Doubles as an upsell surface: "Manage pledges" lives right here.

Watch-outs

  • It's heavy, and on the asset page it competes with the price chart. It may suit the Earn page better.
  • Needs a reduced-motion fallback (digits swap without rolling). That's already handled here.
  • The dark card breaks an otherwise light page. Use it once, sparingly.
03

Payout ring

Earnings are framed around the payout cycle. A ring fills toward payday, one tick per day, while the accrued amount counts up in the centre.

Medium effort
Desktop · 1200
Mobile · 390Scroll inside the phone

Why it works

  • Answers "how much?" and "when?" at a glance.
  • The live countdown builds anticipation, and the projected payout sets expectations.
  • The ring is a familiar pattern, like Apple Fitness or a savings goal.

Watch-outs

  • Only works with a fixed cycle (e.g. monthly). With continuous or daily payouts the ring means less.
  • Takes vertical space on mobile, about 230 px before the details.
04

Pledge ledger

Active earnings broken down by pledge. Each one ticks on its own, with its own term progress, maturity date and daily rate.

Medium effort
Desktop · 1200
Mobile · 390Scroll inside the phone

Why it works

  • Transparent when a user has several pledges with different APYs or terms.
  • Scales to N pledges, and each row is a natural home for actions (top up, unlock, renew).
  • Term progress shows when locked KAU becomes available again.

Watch-outs

  • Lots of ticking rows can feel busy. Consider ticking only the total and showing rows at 6 dp.
  • Long lists need "Show all" collapsing after about 3 rows.

Unified balance

Earnings move into the balance card (your P&L widget). One composition bar shows available, pledged and accruing gold, and accruals tick right next to the balance.

Medium–High effort

Why it works

  • Shows that pledged gold is still the user's gold. It's just working.

Empty state: pledge calculator

Medium effort

09

Buy & pledge + live activity

Users can pledge at the moment they buy. Accruing earnings appear as a live "pending" row at the top of Activity, above the monthly payout rows they'll become.

Medium effort
Desktop · 1200Toggle "Pledge after purchase"
Mobile · 390Close the sheet to see Activity

Why it works

  • Removes a whole flow between buying gold and earning on it.
  • The pending row links "active earnings" to the payout transaction it turns into, so history makes sense.
  • Payout rows give lifetime earnings a verifiable audit trail.

Watch-outs

  • Auto-pledge locks funds, so the term disclosure must be clear. Default the toggle to off if compliance prefers.
  • The activity API has to merge a synthetic pending row with real transactions.

Implementation notes

These apply whichever option you choose.

Precision decides the tick rate

With 6.5 KAU pledged at a blended 5.08%, accrual is about 0.0000000105 KAU/s ($0.0000014/s). The number of decimals decides whether the counter looks alive or frozen:

DisplayChanges everyFeels
KAU · 6 dp (today)~95 sBroken / frozen
KAU · 7 dp~10 sSluggish
KAU · 8 dp~1 sLike a clock ✓
KAU · 10 dp~100× / sBlur, noise
USD · 6 dp~0.7 sLike a clock ✓

Recommendation: choose precision per user, so the hero ticks about once a second whether someone pledges 1 g or 10 kg: dp = clamp(ceil(-log10(kauPerSecond)), 4, 10). Show the first 4 decimals at full weight and dim the rest.

Live engine

  • The API returns a snapshot per pledge: {accrued, ratePerSec, asOf, maturesAt}. The client renders accrued + ratePerSec × (now − asOf) on requestAnimationFrame.
  • Resync every 60 s and when the window regains focus. Never count backwards: if the server value is lower, hold the display until it catches up.
  • Clamp at maturity. Set the rate to 0 when a pledge ends or is unlocked, and stop the pulse.
  • On payout: reset active to 0, bump lifetime and balance with a brief highlight, and add a payout row to Activity. Try 1 day/s above.
  • Only animate on-screen elements (IntersectionObserver). The browser pauses rAF in background tabs.

Motion & accessibility

  • Always use tabular numerals, so digits don't jitter sideways while ticking.
  • prefers-reduced-motion: keep updating once a second, but drop the odometer roll and the pulses.
  • Don't put aria-live on ticking nodes, or screen readers will spam. Expose a rounded aria-label (e.g. "0.0151 KAU earned this cycle") that updates every minute.
  • "Live" needs a text label as well as the green dot. Colour alone isn't enough.
  • Respect the privacy eye: blur every live amount, including the header pill.

Copy

  • Active earnings: "Earned on your pledges since your last payout. Paid into your KAU balance on 1 Nov."
  • Lifetime earnings: "Total earnings paid to your balance to date."
  • Projected payout: "Estimate if your pledges stay the same until 1 Nov. Rates may change."
  • Use "g" or "KAU" consistently. Today the page mixes g (balance) and KAU (earnings).

Open questions for you

  1. Payout cadence. I assumed monthly on the 1st. Is it daily, monthly or at maturity? This decides whether the ring and countdown ideas (03, 01) make sense.
  2. Compounding. Do payouts land as available KAU, or are they auto-added to the pledge?
  3. Rates. Is APY fixed per pledge for its term, or variable? Should a blended APY be shown at all?
  4. Primary unit. Should the hero number be KAU/grams (assumed) or fiat? You can toggle this at the top to compare.
  5. Early unlock. Can a pledge be broken early, and is active earnings then forfeited? If so, the copy needs to say "at risk" somewhere.
  6. The P&L widget mock shows "1.2% APY" and "Available rate 12%". Which rates are real, so the demo data matches?
